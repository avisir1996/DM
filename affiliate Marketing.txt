HOW TO CREATE AN AMAZON AFFILIATE ACCOUNT

STEP 1: OPEN AMAZON 
1. Open https://www.amazon.in/ 
2. Scroll to the bottom of the homepage. 
3. Find “Become an Affiliate” under the footer section. 
4. Click the link.

STEP 2: SIGN UP 
1. Click Sign Up or log in using your Amazon account. 
2. Enter your account details.

STEP 3: ENTER ACCOUNT INFORMATION 
1. Enter Payee Name. 
2. Enter Address.
3. Enter Phone Number. 
4. Select Country.

STEP 4: ADD WEBSITE DETAILS 
1. Enter your WordPress Website URL. 
2. Next.

STEP 5: CREATE AFFILIATE PROFILE 
1. Enter your preferred Store ID. 
2. Describe your website. 
3. Select your website category. 
4. Select how you generate traffic. 
5. Complete the CAPTCHA. 
6. Submit.

STEP 6: OPEN SITESTRIPE SETTINGS 
1. Amazon Associates Dashboard. 
2. Click Tools. 
3. Go to Options and Settings. 
4. Options. 
5. SiteStripe option. 
6. Enable SiteStripe

STEP 7: CREATE AFFILIATE LINKS 
1. Open https://www.amazon.in/ 
2. Search for and open a product. 
3. Use SiteStripe and click Get Link. 
4. Copy the affiliate link.

STEP 8: ADD PRODUCT TO WORDPRESS USING ELEMENTOR 
1. Open WordPress Dashboard. 
2. Go to Pages and select your page. 
3. Click Edit with Elementor. 
4. Add a 2-column Flexbox Container. 
5. Add an Image Widget.
6. Upload  product image. 
7. Link > Paste the Amazon Affiliate Link . 
8. Publish.
