SKU (STOCK KEEPING UNIT):
An SKU is a unique code used by a business to identify and track a specific product or product variation in its inventory.

Examples:
TSH-BLK-M-001
MUG-WHT-002
LAP-DELL-16-001

An SKU can contain letters, numbers and separators such as hyphens. The business decides its own SKU format.

==================================================================

WHY SKU IS IMPORTANT
- Identifies products quickly.
- Helps track stock quantity.
- Reduces confusion between similar products.
- Supports order picking, packing and dispatch.
- Helps with sales and inventory reports.
- Makes product searching easier.
- Helps distinguish size, colour, model or other variations.

==================================================================

SKU EXAMPLE
A store sells the same T-shirt in three colours and three sizes.

Product: Classic T-shirt

Black, Small  -> TSH-BLK-S
Black, Medium -> TSH-BLK-M
Black, Large  -> TSH-BLK-L
White, Small  -> TSH-WHT-S
White, Medium -> TSH-WHT-M
White, Large  -> TSH-WHT-L
Blue, Small   -> TSH-BLU-S
Blue, Medium  -> TSH-BLU-M
Blue, Large   -> TSH-BLU-L

Each distinct variation should have its own SKU when inventory is tracked separately.

Suggested format:
CATEGORY-ATTRIBUTE-MODEL-NUMBER

Examples:
SHIRT-BLK-M-001
SHIRT-WHT-L-002
MUG-RED-350ML-001
PHONE-SAM-A55-128
BOOK-PYTHON-001

SKU IN WOOCOMMERCE — ADD TO A SIMPLE PRODUCT
Products >Add New Product
Enter the product name, description, price and other required details
In the Product data > Inventory tab > SKU
Enter a unique SKU, for example: MUG-WHT-001
If needed, enable Manage stock and enter the stock quantity
Publish

==================================================================

SKU FOR VARIABLE PRODUCTS
Example: T-shirt with size and colour options.

Products > Add New
Product data : Variable product
Attributes and add attributes such as Colour and Size.
Select Used for variations and save the attributes.
Open Variations and create the required combinations.
Expand each variation.
Enter a distinct SKU for each variation, if each one is tracked separately.
Enter each variation's price and stock settings.
Save changes
Test the product page by selecting different variations.

Example:
Black / Small  -> TSH-BLK-S
Black / Medium -> TSH-BLK-M
White / Small  -> TSH-WHT-S

==================================================================

SKU AND STOCK MANAGEMENT
An SKU identifies an item; it does not itself control stock.
==================================================================

To track stock in WooCommerce:
product > Product data > Inventory.
stock Management > Quantity > Save
