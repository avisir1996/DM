HOW TO CREATE A ECOMMERCE WEBSITE USING INFINITYFREE

=============================================================

STEP 1: CREATE AN INFINITYFREE ACCOUNT

https://www.infinityfree.com/
Log in to your InfinityFree account.

=============================================================

STEP 2: CREATE A FREE HOSTING ACCOUNT

Create Account.
Example: myfashionstore.infinityfreeapp.com
Select an available free domain extension.
Complete the account creation process.
Wait until your hosting account is ready.

=============================================================

STEP 3: OPEN THE CONTROL PANEL

Script Installer, find and select WordPress > Install Now.
Choose your domain.
Leave the In Directory field blank to install WordPress on the main website address.
Enter your website name. Example: My Fashion Store
Enter a short website description. Example: Fashion and Lifestyle
