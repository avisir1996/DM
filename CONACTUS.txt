=======================================
HOW TO DESIGN CONACTUS PAGE?
=======================================

PAGE >ADD PAGE>CONTACTUS>PUBLISH

PLUGIN>NINJA FORM>INSTALL>ACTIVATE

PLUGIN>ELEMENTOR>INSTALL>ACT IVATE

NINJAFORM>DASHBOARD>SETTING>EDIT>ADD >PHONE>PUBLISH

NINJAFORM>DASHBOARD>COPY THE SHORTCODE

PAGE>CONTACTUS>EDIT WITH ELEMENTOR>2 COLUMN SHAPE

1ST COLUMN>GOOGLE MAP

2ND COLUMN>SHORTCODE>PASTE SHORTCODE > PUBLISH

FILL ENQUIRY FORM

NINJAFORM>SUBMISSION>SELECT A FORM >SHOW DETAILS
