
COUPONS
A coupon is a promotional code that allows customers to receive a discount or special offer when purchasing products from an online store.

PURPOSE OF COUPONS
 → Attract new customers.
 → Increase sales.
 → Encourage repeat purchases.
 → Promote seasonal offers.
 → Clear old inventory.
 → Promote specific products or categories.


TYPES OF WOOCOMMERCE COUPONS

1. Percentage Discount Provides a percentage discount on eligible products or orders.

Example: Coupon Code: SAVE10 Discount: 10%

If the eligible amount is ₹2,000: Discount = ₹200 Amount after discount = ₹1,800.

==================================================================================

2. Fixed Cart Discount Deducts a fixed amount from the eligible cart total.

Example: Coupon Code: SAVE200 Discount: ₹200

If the cart total is ₹1,500: Amount after discount = ₹1,300.

==================================================================================

3. Fixed Product Discount Deducts a fixed amount from each eligible product unit.

Example: Product price: ₹500 Coupon discount: ₹50 per eligible unit Discounted price per unit: ₹450.

==================================================================================

4. Buy One Get One Free (BOGO) The customer purchases one qualifying product and receives another eligible product free.

Example: Buy one T-shirt for ₹500 and get another eligible T-shirt free.

Note: BOGO offers may require an additional plugin feature.

==================================================================================

5. Automatic Coupon A qualifying coupon is applied automatically when the customer meets the configured conditions.

Example: A customer adds eligible products to the cart and receives a 10% discount without entering a code.

Note: Automatic application may require a compatible coupon plugin.

==================================================================================

HOW TO CREATE A COUPON IN WOOCOMMERCE

Marketing → Coupons.

Add New Coupon.

Enter the Coupon Code Example: WELCOME10

Enter the Coupon Description, if needed: Example: 10% discount for new customers.

Open the General section.

Select the Discount Type:

Percentage discount
Fixed cart discount
Fixed product discount

Enter the Coupon Amount.

10 for a 10% discount.

Coupon Expiry Date Example: 31 December 2026.

Configure Usage Restrictions if required.

Configure Usage Limits if required.

Publish.

Test the coupon on the store's Cart or Checkout page.

PRACTICAL 3: BUY ONE GET ONE FREE (BOGO)

EXAMPLE: Buy one T-shirt for ₹500 and get another eligible T-shirt free.

STEPS:

Smart Coupons  → BOGO → Add New

Enter the offer name. Example: Buy 1 T-shirt, Get 1 Free.

Select the Specific product. Example: T-shirt.

Set the purchase quantity to 1.

Set the discount type to Free or 100%.

Save or activate.

EXPECTED RESULT: The customer receives two eligible T-shirts but pays for only one.

==================================================================================

PRACTICAL 4: AUTOMATIC COUPON APPLICATION

An automatic coupon is a discount applied to an eligible cart without the customer manually entering the coupon code.

EXAMPLE: A customer purchases eligible products worth ₹1,000 and receives a 10% discount automatically.

STEPS:

Marketing → Coupons → Add Coupon → 

Enter the coupon code. Example: AUTO10

Discount Type → Percentage discount.

coupon amount → 10.

Enable Apply Coupon Automatically.

Configure any required product, cart, or usage restrictions.

Set the expiry date if required.

Publish

EXPECTED RESULT: Cart subtotal = ₹1,000 Discount = ₹100 Amount after discount = ₹900 before applicable taxes, shipping, and other adjustments.
